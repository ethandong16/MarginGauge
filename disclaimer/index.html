Estimate limitations

MarginGauge estimates contribution profit and order-linked Payment Account activity using user inputs, supported fee rules, and documented rounding conventions. Actual Etsy statements can differ because of timing, unsupported activity, account-specific adjustments, source changes, or different intermediate rounding.

Supported scope

The first calculator supports selected Etsy.com scenarios for US sellers with a US bank account and USD Payment Account. It does not automatically cover refunds, cancellations, chargebacks, Purchase Protection, reserves, payout timing, Pattern, Square, or every account-specific tax and currency rule.
