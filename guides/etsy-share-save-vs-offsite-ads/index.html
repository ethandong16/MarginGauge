Etsy Share & Save vs Offsite Ads: Fees and Profit

Share & Save rewards an eligible sale you drive with a fee credit. Offsite Ads can charge a fee when Etsy's advertising drives the attributed sale. Treat them as different order outcomes, not percentages to combine.

The standard Share & Save benefit is a 4% refund on an eligible order total after a buyer follows the seller's qualifying link. An attributed Offsite Ads order instead adds a 15% or 12% fee, subject to a $100 per-order cap. Etsy's current rules make these outcomes mutually exclusive for one order.

1. The short answer: identify who drove the attributed order

Share & Save is an optional program for enrolled sellers who share qualifying shop, listing, section, review, or promotion links through approved off-Etsy channels. If a buyer clicks an eligible link and buys from the shop within 30 days, Etsy can issue a fee refund.

Offsite Ads is Etsy's external advertising program. When a qualifying ad click leads to a shop order within its attribution window, Etsy can charge the shop's 15% or 12% Offsite Ads rate. These are not two discounts or fees to add together.

Check the actual order record. MarginGauge can compare scenarios, but it cannot determine which link a buyer clicked last. Use Shop Manager, the order badge, and the Payment Account entry for a completed order.

Joining Share & Save does not erase standard Etsy transaction, payment-processing, or listing charges. It creates a separate refund line when the order qualifies. Likewise, Offsite Ads does not replace the ordinary Etsy fees; it adds an attributed advertising fee.

3. The last qualifying visit can change the outcome

Etsy's Share & Save terms provide a 30-day window after a buyer clicks a seller's qualifying URL. They also state that when an enrolled Offsite Ads seller's buyer later visits through an Offsite Ad and completes the purchase within the window, the transaction is not a Share & Save qualifying transaction and may receive the Offsite Ads fee.

The current Help Center describes this operationally as the buyer's last link before purchase. A last visit from an Offsite Ad makes the order ineligible for Share & Save. Links placed inside Etsy Messages or listing descriptions do not qualify as off-Etsy Share & Save links.

Do not model both on one order. The calculator intentionally makes Share & Save and Offsite Ads mutually exclusive attribution choices.

4. What the 4% refund uses as its base

Etsy's program terms define the qualifying transaction total as displayed listing prices plus buyer-paid shipping and gift wrapping. Paid personalization is included with the listing price. The Help Center describes the calculation as the same order base used for Etsy's transaction fee and excludes taxes.

Standard Share & Save credit = round-cent(4% x eligible pre-tax order total)

For the supported MarginGauge model, the standard credit base is seller order revenue after seller-funded discounts. When an Etsy-funded promotional discount exists, current Etsy guidance says the Share & Save amount uses the eligible order total minus that promotional discount. The calculator therefore keeps the credit separate from seller revenue and other fee lines.

5. One $130 order under five attribution outcomes

Assume one item priced at $120.00 and $10.00 buyer-paid shipping. Product cost is $45.00, packaging is $5.00, and the actual shipping label is $10.00. There is no tax, discount, gift wrap, personalization, Texas seller-fee tax, or allocated Etsy Ads spend.

Seller order revenue is $130.00. Before either program, the existing calculator produces $12.80 of Etsy transaction, payment-processing, and allocated listing fees. Operating costs are $60.00.

That difference is not a promise that seller-driven traffic is free: email software, creators, paid social, content production, and labor are separate marketing costs and should be allocated independently.

6. The 6.5% introductory rate needs seller-specific confirmation

Etsy's current Help Center says new Share & Save participants can earn 6.5% back for their first two weeks. The linked promotion terms limit that rate to invited sellers enrolling for the first time and define an individual 14-day period beginning at enrollment. The 6.5% refund replaces the standard 4% refund during that period; the two rates do not stack.

Use the introductory option only after confirming the invitation and the exact deadline in Shop Manager. The example above contains no Etsy-funded coupon. MarginGauge v1 does not combine the 6.5% introductory rate with an Etsy-funded coupon because the public sources do not resolve that base clearly enough for an automated rule.

7. Compare attribution without hiding marketing cost

Open the Etsy Profit Calculator, enter the full order once, and change only the attribution option. For an existing order, use Etsy's record. For planning, compare no attribution, Share & Save, and the shop's confirmed Offsite Ads tier.

  • Choose standard Share & Save only for an eligible attributed order.
  • Choose the 6.5% introduction only with the invitation and seller-specific end date confirmed.
  • Choose Offsite Ads 12% only after confirming the shop's reduced lifetime tier.
  • Enter external marketing spend separately instead of subtracting it from the Etsy fee or credit.
  • Keep buyer-paid shipping separate from the actual fulfillment cost.
